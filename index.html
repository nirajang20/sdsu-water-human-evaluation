How to score

CorrectnessIs the answer factually accurate?
GroundednessAre factual claims supported by the provided evidence?
CompletenessDoes it include the important information?
RelevanceDoes it directly answer the question?

Rating: 1 Poor · 2 Major issues · 3 Mixed · 4 Good with minor issues · 5 Excellent. Citation formatting is evaluated separately, so don't score the citation syntax.

One question intentionally has no authoritative linked source. For that question, reward an honest acknowledgement of insufficient evidence instead of invented claims.

Evaluator information

Participation and privacy

Your name, email, affiliation, and ratings will be sent to a private university-managed Excel workbook for research analysis. Evaluators are not shown the generating AI models or other evaluators' responses. Participation is voluntary. You may stop before submitting. This portal saves in-progress ratings locally in your browser until you submit. Only submit if you agree to this data use. Contact the study team using the contact information provided with your invitation for questions, withdrawal requests, and retention details.
